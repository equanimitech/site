A principle of the [Equanimity layer](/notes/start-here). Success is internalization. The tool teaches something the user comes to carry on their own, and so needs the tool less.

**The design test**

> The user needs the tool less over time, through internalization. If a user stopped after six months, would we celebrate or panic?
