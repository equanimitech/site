A principle of the [Equanimity layer](/notes/start-here). Who decides what fills your attention? An upstream allocator, like a recommender, decides for you. A downstream tool, like a whiteboard with magnets, leaves the deciding to you.

**The design test**

> The user fills the slots. Recommender systems are upstream allocators; whiteboards with magnets are downstream.
