The first principle of the [Awareness layer](/notes/start-here). Calm technology lives in the periphery. Its default mode is ambient. It moves to the center only when genuinely needed, then returns to the edge.

**The design test**

> Can the user safely ignore this for hours? Does it use the periphery, light, position, a color shift, before demanding focal attention?

<details>
<summary>How it shows up in the work</summary>

- **Zenborg**: area dormancy indicators, attitude whispers, and rhythm signals are all ambient. No push notifications, no badges, no red dots, no modal alerts.
- **keel**: the day's intention and appetite are echoed as a single quiet status line each turn, then silence.
- **Respost**: a card arrives through a channel you already check, so there is no second inbox to manage.

</details>

A budding note, in the calm-technology lineage of Mark Weiser and Amber Case. See also [Bounded Experiences](/notes/bounded-experiences).
