Engagement technology makes us more reactive. It teaches us to want what feels good and reject what feels bad. Equanimitech is the wager that technology can do the opposite: help us observe feelings, good or bad, and look past them to what matters. Tools that make us more intentional, not more reactive.

The framework is nine principles in three layers, each with a design test you can hold a product against.

## Sovereignty

The foundation: your data and tools are yours.

- [Local-First Ownership](/notes/local-first-ownership)
- [Holistic Control](/notes/holistic-control)
- [Modification Rights](/notes/modification-rights)

## Awareness

The moment-to-moment practice: respect attention.

- [Peripheral Presence](/notes/peripheral-presence)
- [Attentional Granularity](/notes/attentional-granularity)
- [Bounded Experiences](/notes/bounded-experiences)

## Equanimity

The long-term outcome: cultivate steadiness.

- [Strategic Friction](/notes/strategic-friction)
- [Fade-by-Design](/notes/fade-by-design)
- [Downstream Allocation](/notes/downstream-allocation)

A design hypothesis, not a validated causal model: the principles create the structural conditions under which equanimity can emerge. They do not produce it. Whether they work is what this studio is here to find out.

The garden grows from here. Most of these notes are seedlings today.
