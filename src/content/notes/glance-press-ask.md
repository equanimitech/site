A seedling. Scaffolding for a fuller note, parked while it is thought through.

**Claim.** Interfaces are not a taxonomy, they are a gradient that work descends as it recurs. Each rung down is cheaper, in cognition and in compute, and more frequent than the one above. "Make everything a chatbot" puts the most expensive surface on the most frequent jobs.

```
        cost          frequency
  ASK    high  ^       rare      novel / one-off / "do this once"
   |            |                conversation, speech, generative inference
   |   cache v  |
  PRESS  mid    |      regular    recurring / "do this often"
   |            |                navigation, classic GUI + cached generative UI
   |  settle v  |                recognition over recall (masks memory)
   v            v
  GLANCE low          constant   ambient / "is everything alright?"
                                 observation, sight, status indicators
```

- **Ask** (conversation, speech, chat): the new, the rare, the one-off. Highest cognitive overhead and highest cost to run. Generative AI makes it possible, but it is the costly tier.
- **Press** (navigation: classic GUI plus cached generative UI): recurring commands. Recognition over recall, the interface masks memory. When an Ask recurs, you cache the generated UI and it crystallizes into a Press. Generate once, navigate thereafter.
- **Glance** (observation, sight, ambient status): constant background checking. The settled, watched but not touched.

**The dynamic.** Cost falls and frequency rises as you go down. Work precipitates: Ask, then cache to Press, then settle to Glance. The discipline is to let recurring work fall to cheaper surfaces, not keep re-asking. This is the old rule made automatic: a recurring conversational command is a smell, it means a missing button, and now the engine mints the button.

**Answers the provocation.** Voice is not a fourth rung; it is a modality of Ask. Chat is where the new lives, not where everything ends up.

**Worked example.** A notification rides all three: it appears through Glance, is dismissed through Press, is resolved through Ask.
