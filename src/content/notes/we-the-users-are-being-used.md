Our attention is consistently exploited for profit by content platforms: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Netflix and the like.

Attention creates engagement. Engagement drives ad revenue. Ad revenue increases profits.

This is the attention economy. Tech giants in a free-for-all to capture our gaze. The tragedy of the commons of human attention. Not only are they competing between themselves, but they're also competing with us.

> "You know, think about it, when you watch a show from Netflix and you get addicted to it, you stay up late at night. We're competing with sleep, on the margin. And so, it's a very large pool of time."
>
> Reed Hastings, Netflix CEO

We, the users, are their biggest competitors. Investing time in ourselves means not spending it in their platforms.

They profit from our distraction.<br />
They profit from our discontentment.<br />
They profit from our compulsive consumption of their content.<br />
They profit. We languish.

> "To flourish means to live within an optimal range of human functioning, one that connotes goodness, generativity, growth, and resilience."
>
> Fredrickson & Losada, 2005

Underneath the garden is one question: "Where will I place my consciousness today?"

[Zenborg](/projects/zenborg) is my exploration into mindful technology. A way to leverage software and AI as a driver for flourishing.
